Theological Reflections on Origen

This paper was written for my early church history class. It was my first foray into writing on the theological exegeses of the early Church Fathers. I was particulary interested in writing on the allegorical interpretations of Origen on the parable of the Good Samaritan; however, the paper’s prompt restricted me to his homilies on Joshua. That said, I was still able to incorporate some of my interest in Jerusalem and Jericho from the parable into my writing. Learning about Origen’s method of reading and interpretation opened me up to a whole new world of Biblical study, and, paired with other exercises in Lectio Divina, will provide me with a lifetime of contemplative material to draw from.
